## Risks & Uncertainty

**Argentina is the weak link (grade C).** Its 2024 net monetary loss of €-206.0m equals about 18.3% of the subsidiary's €1,124.9m total assets. That €-206.0m loss on its own exceeds the whole group's €-195.0m loss; Türkiye (grade A, +2.6%) offsets only €11.0m. A further inflation or FX shock hits this line directly. The loss arises even though Argentina sits in a net monetary liability position, so the simple "liability → gain" heuristic cannot be relied on to cap the downside.

**Model limitation.** This is a calibrated illustrative model, not a reproduction of Unilever's actual subsidiary accounts. It matches the 2024 calibration almost exactly, but the out-of-sample 2025 pass matched sign on only 3 of 4 lines per subsidiary, and magnitudes were off. Operating profit came out at €-1.4m against a disclosed €-54m for Argentina, and the net monetary line at €-113.3m against €-10m for Türkiye. The total-assets line cannot turn negative under this model's mechanics: it produced +€183.7m against a disclosed €-199m for Argentina, and +€46.1m against a disclosed €-20m for Türkiye. Treat any *forward* projection from it with corresponding caution.

**Macro regime shifts.** 2024's positive impacts came from local inflation outpacing managed FX depreciation. Argentina's April 2025 peso float and Türkiye's continued lira depreciation plausibly reversed that relationship (the model's 2025 assumptions encode this reversal as a modeling choice, not a disclosed fact), and the sign of most lines can swing year to year.

**Market pricing.** Public consensus (TipRanks: 5,080p target vs a 4,664.50p close, Moderate Buy) contains no evidence of separating the IAS 29 net monetary line from generic "currency headwind." Paywalled broker notes were not checked. At 2.3% of operating profit (below the 10% materiality threshold), the effect is too small for that blind spot to be a tradeable mispricing.

**Classification.** Hyperinflation status rests on judgment across IAS 29.3's qualitative indicators, not a single bright-line inflation number. The IFRS Interpretations Committee confirmed this in July 2025. Türkiye's inflation is easing toward ~31%, so a future reclassification out of hyperinflationary status is a live possibility that would change the restatement effect in scope. Conversely, a newly hyperinflationary subsidiary would bring a new effect into scope.

**Materiality basis.** The 2.3%-of-operating-profit ratio behind the "immaterial" flag (10% threshold) is measured against an illustrative group base, not Unilever's reported figures.
