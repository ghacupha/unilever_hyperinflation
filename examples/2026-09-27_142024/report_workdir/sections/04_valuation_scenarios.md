## Valuation, Sensitivity & Scenarios

### Three accounting worlds, one set of economics

The model reports the same Argentina and Türkiye operations under three accounting treatments. **World A** is the plain current-rate method. Every line is translated at the current exchange rate and nothing is restated for inflation. This is the "disappearing plant" baseline: as the local currency depreciates, historical-cost fixed assets shrink in euro terms even though the plant is still operating. **World B** is the US GAAP temporal method, known as remeasurement. Non-monetary items stay at historical FX rates, monetary items move to current FX, and the difference goes through the income statement as a *remeasurement* gain or loss. **World C** is what Unilever actually reports under IFRS. IAS 29 first restates the local-currency accounts into current purchasing power, and IAS 21 then translates them at the closing rate. The underlying business is the same in all three worlds. What changes is the accounting, and none of the three is a different valuation of Unilever.

### The numbers

For 2024, Worlds A and B both show revenue of about €60.43bn and operating profit of about €8.49bn. World C shows €60.84bn of revenue and €8.50bn of operating profit. Total assets are €46.01bn in World A, €46.11bn in World B and €46.54bn in World C. World A has no monetary line by construction. World B books a remeasurement loss of about €272m, and World C books a net monetary loss of about €195m.

These gaps are small against a group of Unilever's size. What stands out is the pattern. Moving from A to C raises revenue by roughly €417m and total assets by roughly €539m, but raises operating profit by only about €6m. The reason is that IAS 29 restates revenue and costs by the same inflation factor, so most of the uplift at the top line cancels out at the profit line. The gross revenue and asset figures still move by hundreds of millions.

### IAS 29 impact by subsidiary

In 2024, IAS 29 added about €474m to Argentina's total assets, €230m to its turnover and €10m to its operating profit. Its net monetary result was a loss of about €206m. For Türkiye, the restatement added €65m to total assets and €187m to turnover, reduced operating profit by about €4m, and produced a net monetary gain of about €11m.

Argentina's loss runs against the textbook rule that a net monetary liability position produces a purchasing-power gain. The model solves Argentina as a net monetary *liability* position, yet it still shows a loss. The net monetary line balances the whole restated balance sheet. It therefore also absorbs the inflation-driven growth in restated opening equity and operating profit, and a fast-growing restated equity base can turn an apparent "liability means gain" position into a net loss. The textbook rule is a useful first intuition, but it does not hold as a formula once the full balance sheet is restated.

### 2025 validation

The model was not re-fitted to 2025. It rolls 2024 forward and is then compared with Unilever's disclosed 2025 impacts. For each subsidiary, three of the four lines match in sign:

- **Turnover:** model −€31m vs. disclosed −€90m for Argentina, and −€47m vs. −€16m for Türkiye.
- **Operating profit:** −€1m vs. −€54m for Argentina, and −€3m vs. −€46m for Türkiye. The model understates both.
- **Net monetary loss:** −€24m vs. −€46m for Argentina. For Türkiye the model shows −€113m against a disclosed −€10m, an overshoot of about ten times.

Total assets do not match in sign. The model shows +€184m for Argentina against a disclosed −€199m, and +€46m for Türkiye against −€20m. This is a structural limit of the model, not a calibration miss. Restating non-monetary assets for a rising price index can only increase them, and Worlds A and C divide balance-sheet items by the same closing rate. The model therefore cannot produce a negative total-assets impact. Unilever's negative figure probably reflects items the model does not capture, such as disposals, impairments or a different comparative basis. This remains an open limitation, and the 2025 check should not be read as a clean match.
